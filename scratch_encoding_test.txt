Like Jackson Chourio before him, Made has become one of the shining examples of Milwaukee’s impressive international scouting and internal player development operations since signing for $950,000 out of the Dominican Republic in January 2024. He jumped onto the Top 100 list after slashing .331/.458/.554 in 51 games in the Dominican Summer League in his debut season and then continued to zoom stateside in 2025, opening with Single-A Carolina and rounding out the year at Double-A as an 18-year-old. He hit .285/.379/.413 with six homers, 40 extra-base hits and 47 steals in 115 games across three levels last season.

A switch-hitter, Made is one of the twitchiest prospects in baseball, relying on quick bat speed from both sides of the plate. He ran almost exactly even platoon splits in ’25, and while his six homers don’t exactly jump off the page, his raw power continues to grow as he ages. Should he lift the ball more with additional physical maturity, there’s the potential for 25-30 homers down the line to go with a good average, considering the approach and quality of contact.

Made uses that quick-twitch nature on the basepaths as a plus runner, and since he’s signed, he’s proven capable of making highlight moves with good range and a quick release in the field at shortstop. Coaches and evaluators close to him were pleased with how he improved his consistency on more mundane plays at the six, and he could be above average there in time. Milwaukee has given Made time at second and third base early in his career but is expected to keep prioritizing shortstop, where Made could be a franchise player at a premium position.
