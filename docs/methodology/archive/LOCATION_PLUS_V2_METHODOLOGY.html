What It Measures

Location+ measures command quality — where a pitch is thrown relative to the strike zone, independent of what the pitch is. A called-strike on a corner against a same-hand batter in a hitter's count earns more than a ball off the plate; Location+ captures how much value a pitcher extracts purely from pitch placement.

It fills the deliberate gap left by Stuff+ (physical shape only, excludes plate location) and Tunneling+ (sequence context only, excludes plate location). Together the three models give orthogonal decompositions of why a pitch was effective.

Why v2? The previous live model (location_plus_v1.txt, single regressor) had two critical failures: (1) a sign-flip (ERA r = −0.257, xFIP r = −0.507 — higher score = worse pitcher), and (2) a stability collapse (YoY r = 0.009 for 2024→2025, std = 3.07). v2 fixes both: ERA r = +0.208, xFIP r = +0.349, YoY r = 0.361 pooled, std = 9.99.

Architecture

Same five-classifier decomposed architecture as Tunneling+ v2: each classifier predicts one outcome probability, combined via a fixed run-value table into per-pitch xRV, then aggregated to pitcher-season and plus-scaled.

ClassifierTargetLOSO AUCNote
xSwing%did the batter swing?0.848Location is highly informative about swing decisions
xCS%called strike (no swing)?0.981Near-perfect: plate_x/z directly determines strike zone

Each classifier is LGBMClassifier(n_estimators=500, num_leaves=31, learning_rate=0.05, min_child_samples=50, subsample=0.8, colsample_bytree=0.8). Leave-one-season-out CV on 2015–2025 for model selection; retrained on full 2015–2026 for live inference.

Combine Formula

Same as Tunneling+ v2. Per-pitch xRV:

xRV = p_sw × (p_wh × rv_whiff + (1−p_wh) × (p_fo × rv_foul + (1−p_fo) × (p_bip · bip_rvs))) + (1−p_sw) × (p_cs × rv_cs + hbp_rate × rv_hbp + clip(1−p_cs−hbp_rate,0,1) × rv_ball)

Fixed run values from a 2021–2025 league-average table. HBP rate = 0.51%.

Features

7 features total — the minimal set that captures where a pitch was thrown and the context that governs the strike zone:

FeatureTypeDescription
plate_xnumericHorizontal plate position (feet from center, catcher's perspective)
plate_znumericVertical plate position (feet from ground)
ballsnumericBall count at pitch delivery
strikesnumericStrike count at pitch delivery
pitch_typecategoricalPitch type (needed — whiff rates differ by pitch type at same location)
standcategoricalBatter handedness (L/R)
seasoncategoricalSeason year

Exclusions by design: no pitcher identity (generalizes across pitchers), no pitch shape (that's Stuff+), no sequence geometry (that's Tunneling+).

Training Data

v1/v2 Cross-Correlation

Location+ v1 and Location+ v2 cross-correlate at r ≈ −0.48 to −0.60 (negative), confirming the old and new versions rank pitchers in opposite directions — the sign-flip was a fundamental inversion, not a rescaling. Location+ v2 × Tunneling+ v2 = r = +0.617 (both correctly signed).

Known Issues

  • No SHAP breakdown in live API: decomposed-classifier architecture has no single SHAP model to query. The /pitcher/{id} endpoint returns a shap_note field; the frontend should display this gracefully (pending fix).
  • Pitch Locations chart orientation: chart on the player page may show RHH/LHH backwards vs. Baseball Savant orientation. Deferred investigation.
  • YoY stability: r = 0.361 (pooled) is weaker than Stuff+ v3 (r = 0.907) — expected given that command is a more volatile skill than physical pitch quality.
