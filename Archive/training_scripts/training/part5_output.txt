=== 4. Season-level vs. rolling window note ===
[13:58:21]   Current implementation: arm_angle_std / release_extension_std are computed
[13:58:21]   as the season-long standard deviation per (pitcher_id, season) pair.
[13:58:21]   This means the _std value for every pitch a pitcher throws in a season
[13:58:21]   is identical — it's a season-level aggregate, not a pitch-level running stat.
[13:58:21]   Rolling window alternative: compute std over last N pitches.
[13:58:21]   Advantage: captures mid-season delivery changes (e.g., mechanical adjustment).
[13:58:21]   Disadvantage: introduces look-ahead bias unless carefully implemented.
[13:58:21]   Recommendation: defer. Season-level aggregation is the honest, simple choice
[13:58:21]   and aligns with the 'year-over-year' framing of Deception+ as a whole.
