Career Stats

Season-by-season history across all data in the pipeline (2021–present). Players who changed teams mid-season appear as one row per team.

Select a player to view their career stats.
Pitcher model scores (Stuff+, Tunneling+, Location+) are season-level averages from the same models used throughout the dashboard. Deception+ shown for 2025 only (current report season). IP and xFIP are computed from Statcast pitch data; ERA is not available (Statcast carries no official scorer rulings). AVG/OBP/SLG/wRC+ for batters sourced from player_advanced_stats where available.
