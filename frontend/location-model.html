Location+ Model

v3. Measures where a pitch ended up and how valuable that location was, given the count and batter handedness — nothing about the pitch's physical shape (that's Stuff+), delivery (Deception+), or relationship to other pitches in the arsenal (Tunneling+). Key v3 fix: the run-value table is now count-specific (a called strike on 3-2 is worth ~7.8x more than on 0-0 in run expectancy terms — v2 used a single pooled mean and missed this entirely). Training excludes 2020; the 60-game season is scored by averaging model predictions from season=2019 and season=2021 categorical inputs.

ℹ️
How to read this number
100 = league average for that season. Each 10 points is one standard deviation. Location+ needs ~300 pitches before it is reliable at the pitcher level (split-half r ≥ 0.81 at 300+ pitches per half). Read leaderboard positions below that pitch count with real caution.
