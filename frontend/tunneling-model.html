Tunneling+ Model

v3, the current standard. A single trained model (LightGBM, no hand-built composite) that scores every real pitch using up to 6 pitches of real, same-at-bat sequence history: for each pitch back (lag 1-6), how far apart it and the current pitch were at this pitcher's own commit point (reused from v2 - the distance from the plate reached 200ms before arrival, computed from that pitcher's own fastest fastball-family pitch, not a fixed distance for every pitcher) and at the plate, plus a shape-break term and the prior pitch's type. Unlike v1/v2, same-type prior pitches are NOT excluded - a pitcher's 2nd straight fastball is real information, not noise to drop. The model has no separate "pair score" at all - it's trained directly against real run-value outcomes (delta_run_exp) and scored 100 = league average, same convention as every other model on this site. A tighter score spread than Stuff+ is expected here, not a flaw: tunneling is a real but inherently smaller lever a pitcher can pull independently, compared to raw pitch quality.

ℹ️
How to read this number
100 = league average for that season. Each 10 points is one standard deviation from that season's league-wide average - a score of 110 means a pitcher graded roughly 1 standard deviation better than average that season, not "10% better." Scores are z-scored fresh within each season, so the same score in two different years means the same relative standing, not necessarily the same raw value.

Lag-Slot Importance (SHAP)

Mean absolute SHAP contribution per pitch-back (commit + plate + shape-break distances, plus prior pitch type) - how much each lag slot actually moves a prediction, on average. Falls off with distance back, as expected: what happened 1 pitch ago matters more than 6 pitches ago.
