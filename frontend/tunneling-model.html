Tunneling+

Grades how indistinguishable a pitcher's pitches look from each other, from release all the way to the plate, using up to 6 pitches of real same-at-bat sequence history. A tighter score spread than Stuff+ is expected here, not a flaw: tunneling is a real but inherently smaller lever than raw pitch quality.

ℹ️
How to read this number
100 = league average for that season. Each 10 points is one standard deviation. Scores are z-scored fresh within each season, so the same score in two different years means the same relative standing, not necessarily the same raw value. A score of 110 means roughly 1 SD better than that season's average, not "10% better."

Lag-slot importance (SHAP)

Mean absolute SHAP contribution per pitch-back (commit + plate + shape-break distances, plus prior pitch type): how much each lag slot actually moves a prediction, on average. Falls off with distance back, as expected: what happened 1 pitch ago matters more than 6 pitches ago.

Out-of-sample predictive validity

A model trained on every season except 2024, scored on 2024's pitches, correlated against each pitcher's real 2025 outcomes: a genuine out-of-sample check, not in-sample fit.
