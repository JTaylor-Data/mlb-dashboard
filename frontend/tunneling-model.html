Tunneling+ Model

v1, first pass - a self-directed model, not built to any outside spec. Measures pitch-pair tunneling: how close together two of a pitcher's pitch types are (in inches, full pairwise average across every real pitch of each type - not a distance between averages) at the point a hitter has to commit to swinging (~23.5 ft from the plate), versus how far apart they actually end up at the plate. Tunneling+ is the geometric mean of Decision Sim+ (close at the decision point) and Plate Sep+ (different late), each scaled 100=league average, same convention as Stuff+/wRC+. A pair needs to be reasonably good at both to score well; one strong half can't fully carry a weak one. Release Sim+ is also shown (close at release) but isn't part of the score - tested as a third factor and dropped after it measurably weakened the model's validated predictive power, see the PDF's Section 3.3. Pairs involving the four-seam or sinker (a hitter's primary read) count double toward a pitcher's headline score - shaded pairs in the arsenal table below. Physics-derived from Statcast's own trajectory fit (the same constant-acceleration model this site's VAA metric already uses), not a proxy or a black box - see the validation results below and the full methodology PDF for the complete reasoning.
