How to read this number
100 = league average for that season. Each 10 points is one standard deviation. Scores are z-scored fresh within each season, so the same score in two different years means the same relative standing, not necessarily the same raw value. A score of 110 means roughly 1 SD better than that season's average, not "10% better."
