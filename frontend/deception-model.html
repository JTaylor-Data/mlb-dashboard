Deception+ v2

Grades release mechanics (arm slot, extension, release point) as predictors of outcomes, independent of pitch velocity, movement, or spin. Scores are cross-fitted: every pitcher's grade comes from models that never trained on their own pitches.

Arm slot Release extension Release position Delivery consistency
100 = league average for that season. Each 10 points is one standard deviation above or below league average: a score of 120 means a pitcher's mechanics graded roughly 2 SD more deceptive than the league that year. Scores are standardized within each season, so the same score in different years means the same relative standing. Minimum 300 pitches to qualify; below-threshold pitchers are shrunk toward average and marked unqualified.

The evolution

  • 1v1: proof-of-concept single-season model, per-request scoring. Split-half r=0.998 was an artifact (consistency features computed on the full season before splitting); body features (height, weight) could memorize pitcher identity.
  • 2v2: cross-fitted ensemble (pitcher-grouped folds). Removed body identity features (height, weight, arm_angle_std, release_extension_std, release_consistency) per pre-registered identifiability rules. No per-request scoring, all scores pre-computed. Season selector enabled (2021-2026).
  • 3v2b (current): dropped the season feature per a pre-registered inconclusive_means_drop rule (ablation found it did not help). Same cross-fitted ensemble architecture otherwise. Scores 2021-2026.
