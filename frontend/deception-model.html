Deception+ Model

v1, proof-of-concept. Measures how much a pitcher's body and release consistency - arm angle, extension, release point, height/weight, and the pitch-to-pitch variability of each - predict outcomes, gradient-boosted (LightGBM) and deliberately excluding pitch type, velocity, movement, and pitcher identity, so it can't just re-derive Stuff+ under a new name. This was originally meant to also cover windup timing, leg lift, and time held in the set position - see the excluded-features note below and the full methodology PDF for why those aren't included yet, and what was actually tried to get them.
