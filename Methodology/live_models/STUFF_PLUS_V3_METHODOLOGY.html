1. What It Measures

Stuff+ grades the physical shape of a pitch — velocity, movement, spin, and how these compare to the pitcher's own fastball. It explicitly excludes location, count, and pitcher identity. Two hard rules enforced at training time via assert_no_identifiers():

  • No plate_x / plate_z / balls / strikes — those belong to Location+ (command/sequencing, not pitch quality)
  • No pitcher_id / game_id — a model that sees identity memorizes "good pitcher" rather than learning why a pitch shape works

100 = league average that season. Each 10 points = 1 standard deviation. A 115 means the pitch quality is 1.5 SD above average.

3. Key Feature Details

VAA Residual

Raw VAA is confounded with plate height and velocity. fit_vaa_expectation() fits vaa ~ plate_z + plate_z² + release_speed per CV fold (not leaking held-out season). The residual captures "flatter or steeper than physics predicts" — the genuine pitch-quality component of approach angle.

axis_differential (SSW proxy)

The gap between "what spin axis would pure Magnus physics need to produce this pitch's real movement" and the actual measured spin_axis. A proxy for seam-shifted-wake / active spin. Calibrated empirically from FF population data (not from documented conventions, to avoid silent sign bugs). SHAP importance ranks 4th of 15; EDoF higher than hb/ivb — confirms distinct signal, not collinearity.

Primary-Fastball Separation

For each pitcher-season, the highest-usage FF/SI/FC is the primary fastball reference. Every other pitch gets separation features (velo_sep, hb_sep, ivb_sep) against that reference. Primary pitch itself gets true NaN (not zero) — LightGBM handles natively. Ablation confirmed 1-slot outperforms 2-slot and 3-slot on YoY stability (r²: 0.754 vs. 0.692 vs. 0.720). More slots add noise without predictive gain.

Pitch-Type Exclusions

PO, UN, EP excluded from training AND scoring — these score above mean predicted run value (not real competitive attempts). KN deliberately kept despite known modeling difficulty.

5. Scoring Pipeline

Step 1 — Empirical-Bayes shrinkage. Each pitcher's raw per-pitch-type mean is shrunk toward the pitch type's league mean. Shrinkage constants K (in units of pitches) are estimated fresh per season via method-of-moments variance decomposition — never hardcoded. In practice K = 0.4–2.8 for all well-populated pitch types (stabilizes almost immediately).

Step 2 — Plus-scale. 100 − 10 × (shrunk_pred − season_mean) / season_std, computed within each season. Overall score = usage-weighted average across arsenal.

SHAP breakdown uses unshrunk predictions — shrinkage is a statistical correction, not attributable to any feature, so it's excluded from per-feature attribution.

Pitcher identity excludedBy design — generalizes to unseen pitch shapes, but cannot account for deceptive mechanics.
