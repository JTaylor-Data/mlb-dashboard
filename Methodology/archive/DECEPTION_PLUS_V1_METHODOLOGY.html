1. What It Measures

Deception+ grades pitcher delivery mechanics and body — does something about how a pitcher moves and releases predict good outcomes, independent of pitch shape? It captures what Stuff+ deliberately excludes: release consistency, arm slot, extension, and body size.

100 = league average. 10 points = 1 SD. Covers 2021–2025 (arm_angle data started mid-2020; only 75.1% populated in 2020 — not enough to extend further back). The live site hardcodes 2025 — no season selector is wired up (REPORT_SEASON = 2025 in api/routers/deception_report.py).

Excluded by design: pitch shape (Stuff+ territory), plate location/count (Location+ territory), arsenal sequencing (Tunneling+ territory), pitcher identity.

Overlap with Stuff+ v2 (resolved in v3): arm_angle, release_extension, release_pos_x_as, release_pos_z were in both Stuff+ v2 and Deception+ v1. Stuff+ v3 removed all four — these are now Deception+-exclusive. This dropped the Stuff+/Deception+ cross-correlation from 0.535 to 0.377.

3. Key Feature Interaction: arm_angle + release_pos_z

Don't read arm_angle SHAP in isolation. arm_angle and release_pos_z are correlated at r = 0.79 league-wide. The combined SHAP curve shows: submarine delivery (~−60°) is strongly rewarded (+14 pts), over-the-top (~70°) is penalized (−3 to −6 pts), and the normal range is roughly flat. A naive read of arm_angle alone makes extreme submariners look penalized — that's the confound. Example: Tyler Rogers 2025 — arm_angle SHAP: −3.5 pts, release_pos_z SHAP: +17.7 pts, combined effect strongly positive, overall score 133.5 (elite).

6. What Can't Be Measured (Confirmed)

The following features were investigated and confirmed NOT obtainable from any accessible data source. They would require frame-by-frame motion-capture data Statcast doesn't publish:

  • Delivery length (time from first movement to release)
  • Leg-lift timing
  • Time held in set position
  • Ball concealment in glove

A computer-vision pipeline (pitcher_cv/deception_v4/) was built to extract these from Statcast broadcast video using MediaPipe + YOLO. Structurally complete but not wired into the live model. Foot-plant detection (a key phase-timing signal) unsolved at last session. Not a priority until/unless there's a clear path to production quality.

8. Live Site Limitations

The live site only displays 2025 scores. REPORT_SEASON = 2025 is hardcoded in api/routers/deception_report.py. The model is season-agnostic and covers 2021–2025, but no UI season selector is wired up. A league-wide scorer was built for diagnostics but not merged into the live runtime — deception_model.py scores one pitcher at a time on demand.
