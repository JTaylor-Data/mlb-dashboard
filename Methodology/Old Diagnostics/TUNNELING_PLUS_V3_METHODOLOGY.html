1. What It Measures

Tunneling+ grades how indistinguishable a pitcher's pitches look out of the hand before they separate near the plate — the "deception through similarity" dimension of pitching. v3 learns this directly from real outcomes (run value) using lag-context features, rather than computing an explicit geometric similarity ratio.

100 = league average that season. Each 10 points = 1 SD. Score spread is deliberately tight (~95–104 typical range) — tunneling is a real but smaller lever than raw stuff quality.

Excluded by design: absolute plate coordinates (plate_x/plate_z), pitcher identity, physical pitch shape (those belong to Location+ and Stuff+).

The key shift: v3 has no "pair" concept at the scoring level. Every pitch from the 2nd of an at-bat onward is one training row, described by its relationship to up to 6 prior pitches in that at-bat. The model predicts that pitch's own run-value outcome directly.

3. The Commit Point (Reused from v2)

For each pitcher-season: find the hardest FF/SI/FC by effective_speed, compute where it was 200ms before arriving using that pitch's real trajectory. That distance = the pitcher's commit point — applied to every pitch they throw that season.

Commit-point distance grows with velocity (harder thrower = more ground covered at a fixed time). A pitcher with no qualifying FF/SI/FC gets no commit point and is absent from v3 output.

Source file: training/tunneling_v2_artifacts/200ms/pitcher_season_commit_point.csv — 9,348 pitcher-seasons, covers 2015–2026. v3 reads this directly, does not re-derive it.

4. Feature Set (28 Total)

For each real pitch (2nd of at-bat onward), look back up to 6 real prior pitches in that at-bat:

Feature (×6 lag slots)What it captures
lagN_commit_distanceDistance between this pitch and the N-back pitch at this pitcher's own commit point
lagN_plate_distanceDistance between them at the plate
lagN_shape_breakHow much the pair "pops apart early / tracks back late" vs. linear interpolation
lagN_pitch_typeWhat pitch type was thrown N pitches back (categorical)

6 lag slots × 4 features = 24 lag features. Plus 4 current-pitch categoricals: pitch_type, p_throws, stand, season = 28 total. A lag slot beyond the at-bat's actual pitch count is true NaN (never zero-filled) — LightGBM handles natively. No pitch-type-pair identity feature exists — the model never sees "FF vs. CH", only sequential pitch context.

7. ⚠ Known Critical Issue — Sign Flip

Tunneling+ v3 ranks pitchers in the wrong direction relative to ERA/xFIP. This was discovered in the 2026-09-21 validation study (Parts A+C). The live Location+ v1 model has the same problem. The v2 candidates for both models fix this. Evidence: training/validate_v2_complete.log, consolidated validation report (artifact). Promotion decision is pending — see HANDOFF.md.
